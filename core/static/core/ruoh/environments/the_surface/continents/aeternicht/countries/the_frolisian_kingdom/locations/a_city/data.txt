Aur City stands as one of Frolysia’s largest urban centers, characterized by towering factories, dense residential districts, and extensive underground infrastructure.

Recent investigations suggest the city was constructed over a much older settlement. Structural anomalies and unexplained energy readings have been detected below the lower districts, prompting increased RUOH surveillance.
